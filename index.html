Worldbuilding / foundations

Three layers of life.

NEXUS is a persistent virtual world layered over a separate modern reality. Each world has its own atmosphere, possibilities and everyday rhythms.

01 — VIRTUAL WORLD

VR Glassy World

A futuristic fantasy environment of glassy towers, bridges, holograms, silent vehicles and lively markets. Floating homes, sky trains and enormous creatures moving through the sky are part of its ordinary wonder. Players explore, trade, train, meet others and build independent lives.

02 — ADVENTURING WORLD

Hunting & Adventuring

Beyond the city atmosphere lie forests, mountains, deserts, frozen lands, volcanoes, oceans and ancient ruins. The overworld is not itself a dungeon. Dungeons are separate portal-spaces with their own fantasy environments and ecosystems; not every creature is hostile.

03 — REALITY

The everyday world

Modern life continues separately from NEXUS. School, home, family, teachers and ordinary routines remain part of the wider story. NEXUS is popular, but it does not replace reality or make every person exceptional.
